Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pikvora (“Pikvora,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit this website or use our locally hosted, personal content-planning tool. Review and update this policy before launch so it accurately reflects the features you enable and the data you actually collect.

1. Scope

This policy covers the public Pikvora website and the personal-use tool we may operate to create, organize, schedule, or review content for our own Pinterest account. It does not replace the privacy policies of Pinterest, Amazon, Google, or other third-party services.

2. Information we may process

Depending on enabled features, Pikvora may process:

  • Authorization information: Pinterest account identifiers and OAuth tokens returned when the tool is connected to Pinterest.
  • Content information: Pin images, titles, descriptions, board information, publishing schedules, and account analytics accessed or created by the tool.
  • Product and affiliate information: Product details, destination URLs, and affiliate links used to prepare content.
  • Technical information: Hosting or server logs such as request time, IP address, browser information, and error details, where collected by the host.

We do not intend to collect sensitive personal information through this website. Please do not send us sensitive information.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to operate and secure the website, generate and organize content, publish or schedule authorized Pins, display permitted account reporting, troubleshoot errors, and comply with applicable law and platform requirements.

4. Pinterest and other third-party services

If you authorize Pikvora to connect to Pinterest, the tool will access only data and actions permitted by the permissions granted and Pinterest's API terms. Pinterest handles information under its own policies. We do not sell Pinterest account data or use Pinterest data for purposes prohibited by Pinterest.

If Gemini or another AI provider is enabled, prompts and content included in those prompts may be sent to that provider for processing. Send only information necessary for the task and review the provider's terms and privacy practices. Affiliate links may take you to third-party retailer websites, which have their own policies.

5. Storage, security, and retention

The personal-use tool is intended to run locally. Depending on configuration, its database, logs, generated assets, and authorization tokens may be stored on the device or environment where it runs. The public website may be hosted separately by a third-party static hosting provider.

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the setup to protect information, including restricting access to local files and keeping API keys and OAuth tokens out of public source repositories. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, unless a longer period is required by law. Actual retention depends on local database, backup, and log settings. Delete local records and revoke app access when no longer needed.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by services you choose to connect, such as Pinterest, an AI provider, or a hosting provider, to deliver requested features. We may disclose information if required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights or security.

7. Cookies and analytics

This starter website does not intentionally set advertising cookies or run third-party analytics by default. The hosting provider may collect technical logs. If analytics, cookies, tracking pixels, or other services are added, update this policy before enabling them.

8. Your choices and deletion

You can stop using the tool, remove locally stored data, and revoke Pikvora's access from your Pinterest account settings. Revoking access may stop connected features from working. Some records may remain in backups or provider logs for a limited period, subject to their retention practices and applicable law.

9. Children's privacy

Pikvora is not directed to children and is not intended for use by children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or tool changes. The effective date at the top will be revised when changes are published.
